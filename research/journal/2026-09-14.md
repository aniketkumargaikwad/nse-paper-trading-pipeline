## Daily washout entry with regime-gated exits: ride confirmed uptrends

**Training outcome:** 916 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 91528410

**Lessons:** 1) Judge only on combos_beating_hold; net PnL in a 400-1500% drift window is noise. 2) An exit rule that cannot stay in a compounder will lose the war regardless of hit rate - asymmetric penalty on the right tail. 3) Identical results across 5m-60m proves the extra data is unused; either commit to the daily bar or build a signal that actually reads intrabar structure. 4) Any future version needs a per-symbol trend/compounder filter (hold-and-do-nothing on strong uptrends, trade only the range-bound high-vol names) or it will keep recreating this exact profile. 5) Test against hold on a per-symbol basis from the start, not as a post-hoc column.
