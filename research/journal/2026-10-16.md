# Research notes — 2026-10-16

## Leader 20-day volume breakout, held on a 2-close 100-SMA break / 5-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 157 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 17975451

**Lessons:** A trend entry with exits after about 100 days adds value in cyclical and falling stocks but gives up the long compounding runs, so the edge against holding is about zero. Future ideas need an entry filter or regime filter that cuts losing years like 2011 and keeps the drop from a high under 30%. They also need to hold winners longer through a trailing exit rather than a fixed one, and rank signals instead of taking them first-come. That ranking matters because roughly 79% of signals are skipped.
