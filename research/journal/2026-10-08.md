# Research notes — 2026-10-08

## Leader 1.5-ATR pullback reclaim, 2-10 day swing on ATR stop/target

**Training outcome:** 94 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 6612

**Lessons:** Short ~15-day swing trades with frequent entry and exit get eaten by costs on NSE daily data. The basket result is close to zero, and the edge is significantly negative, not just noisy. The big compounders (BAJFINANCE, ASTRAL, KEI, TRENT) were traded in and out and missed nearly all their gains. Reaching 5-7% a month needs a rule that stays invested in strong, persistent trends, trades much less often, and has a market-regime filter to limit drawdowns.

## ADX-confirmed trend dip, reclaim-day entry, 2x/3x ATR bracket, 10-bar cap

**Training outcome:** 110 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 779538

**Lessons:** Short fixed-horizon swing exits cap winners in a strongly trending universe where holding returns 1.75% a month. To beat holding, a rule must stay invested longer and ride trends, with exits driven by trend breaks rather than time. Low slot use is the main reason for the idle-capital drag. Even with full use, the active excess is negative, so the entry logic itself lacks an edge.

## Calm 6-month leader at a 20-day high, held on a 2-close EMA50 break / 5-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 111 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 3143231

**Lessons:** An entry this selective gives up too much of the long-term drift in compounding Indian midcaps. Positive P&L in bull bursts is not an edge when active excess is about zero. Clustered signals waste capacity in strong regimes and leave capital idle otherwise. A 5-7% monthly target needs far more time in the market plus real selection skill, which a sparse trigger cannot deliver. Two versions are left, and tweaking this idea is unlikely to turn a negative edge_t positive.

## Volume-confirmed 20-day high in rising 200-SMA, fail-fast cull, ATR-trailed swing

**Training outcome:** 94 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 130696

**Lessons:** Short swing holds of 8-15 days with frequent re-entry give up the long compounding trends of winners like BAJFINANCE and EICHERMOT, and fees consume almost all the gross edge. The rule suits falling or range-bound stocks, not quality compounders. Reaching 5-7% a month needs a rule that stays invested in strong trends much longer and trades far less. Drawdown control at the account level, such as a market regime filter that keeps out of bear years, is required to qualify. Further tweaks of this short-swing idea are unlikely to reverse a t-stat of -4.3.

## Williams %R oversold-to-overbought thrust, ATR-bracketed 2-10 day swing

**Training outcome:** 94 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 403612

**Lessons:** Short swing holds of 4-10 days on daily bars leave capital idle about 70% of the time, and they cannot keep up with holding on compounding winners (BAJFINANCE, ASTRAL, KEI). The edge appears only as downside avoidance on structurally declining stocks. Future ideas should stay invested longer, for example with trend-following exits or regime filters that hold through uptrends, and should cut turnover so fees eat less. They should also judge excess while active before chasing raw PnL.
