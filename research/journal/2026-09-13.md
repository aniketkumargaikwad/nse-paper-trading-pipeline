# Research notes — 2026-09-13

## Prior-bar high breakout in an EMA20>EMA50 uptrend on above-average volume

**Training outcome:** 9 of 1164 combinations profitable after fees, net -204998191

**Lessons:** Judge every combo against its own buy-and-hold, not against zero; on this survivorship-heavy 2023-25 universe a flat PnL bar is meaningless. Split gross from net early - gross was already negative here, so no amount of fee tuning, timeframe pruning, or dropping 5m could have rescued it. Trade count per combo is a fast sanity check: ~910 trades and 25% wins is a churn signature, and 0 trades on 'day' should fail loudly rather than be reported as a result. Worst_dip of 100% means position sizing has no survival constraint. Finally, 9 profitable out of 1,164 is below what random entries would give - there is nothing in this signal worth another version.

## Volume-confirmed Bollinger breakout in an uptrend (ATR risk)

**Training outcome:** 213 of 1164 combinations profitable after fees, net -78914497

**Lessons:** 1) Judge every combo against buy-and-hold on the same symbol and window; absolute PnL in a 417%-average-return market is meaningless. 2) Check gross PnL first — if gross is negative, tuning fees, timeframes or filters cannot save the idea. 3) A clean monotonic improvement across timeframes usually just means fewer trades, not better ones; verify per-trade gross edge instead of the bucket total. 4) Sub-hour timeframes on NSE equities need a per-trade gross edge well above round-trip cost before they are even worth backtesting. 5) 90%+ worst dips should hard-fail a combo regardless of PnL.

## EMA20 reclaim inside an EMA20>EMA50 uptrend, ATR stops

**Training outcome:** 53 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net -157215705

**Lessons:** 1) Judge every result against hold_return_pct on the same symbol and window; by that bar zero combos here passed, so the 'top' table was misleading me and should be reported as excess-over-hold, not raw net PnL. 2) Average hold return of ~418% across training years means these are strongly trending bull-market years: any long-biased signal will show winners that are pure beta, so this training window cannot validate an idea without a hold benchmark or a short side. 3) Gross PnL being negative before fees is the decisive test; check it first and abandon early rather than tuning costs or filters. 4) Trade count scaling inversely with profitability across 5m/25m/30m/60m means the signal decays to noise intraday. 5) A timeframe with 0 trades over 198 combos must be treated as a broken condition and fixed before the run, not scored as flat.
