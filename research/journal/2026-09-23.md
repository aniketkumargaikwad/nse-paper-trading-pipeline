# Research notes — 2026-09-23

## Daily 50-day breakout with a 12% give-back brake and half-size de-risking

**Training outcome:** 608 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 7185083

**Lessons:** 1) Fee drag decides everything at this trade frequency - 200k trades at 25% win rate cannot pay for itself; anything below 60m should be dropped outright rather than re-tuned. 2) A positive net_pnl total is meaningless when avg_excess_active_pct is negative across all six timeframes; the rule is a worse way to own the same stocks. 3) 76-84% of signals are skipped for lack of slots, so the account result is largely a queueing artifact - fixing entry logic without ranking which signal gets a slot changes little. 4) The idea only ever works on high-volatility momentum names; applying it to a broad 194-stock universe guarantees the quality compounders drag it under. 5) Drawdowns above 30% on the day account mean position sizing/exit risk, not signal quality, is the binding constraint.
