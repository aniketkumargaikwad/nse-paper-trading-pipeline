# Research notes — 2026-10-01

## Leader tight-pause breakout, 2-10 day ATR-bracketed swing with fail-fast cull

**Training outcome:** 48 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 153497

**Lessons:** A rare-signal swing entry on daily bars cannot reach 10 trades a month or 5-7% a month on a ten-slot account. Loosening its threshold would only add more trades with no edge, and fees already eat a quarter of gross. The next idea needs to fire often (many signals a day across 194 stocks) and stay invested much longer, so slot use is high. One option is ranking stocks by trend or momentum and riding the leaders for weeks, which would close the large idle-capital gap against holding.

## Uptrend RSI-40 reclaim in 6-month leaders, held on a 50-SMA break / 5-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 70 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 718903

**Lessons:** One-day swing exits on daily bars cannot reach a 5-7% month. Edge per trade is too small against fees, and idle capital loses to holding in a market that rose about 1.8% a month. Mostly avoiding losers is not enough; the rule has to stay invested in the stocks that trend for months. Three versions have not produced positive active excess, so tuning this idea further is unlikely to help.

## Uptrend RSI-40 reclaim with own-stock regime brake (index filter not expressible)

**Training outcome:** 97 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 268836

**Lessons:** Short fixed-hold swing exits on daily bars cannot match buy-and-hold on Indian large and midcaps, whose long-run drift is huge. The edge has to come from staying invested longer in trending names, using trend-following exits instead of time stops, and cutting turnover so fees stop eating the gross. Slot use near 50% alongside 30% skipped signals means entries should be ranked, not taken first-come. Treat top-table winners on declining stocks as luck, not edge.
