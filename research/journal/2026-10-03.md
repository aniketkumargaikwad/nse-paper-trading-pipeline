# Research notes — 2026-10-03

## Momentum-leader inside-day break, 2-10 day ATR-bracketed swing

**Training outcome:** 83 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net -240916

**Lessons:** Short-hold swing entries on daily bars are eaten by costs, and they forfeit the trend that drives Indian large and mid caps (holding averaged +1.76% a month). Beating a 5-7% monthly target needs capital deployed most of the time in the strongest uptrends, with few, long-held trades. The large negative t-stat says tweaking this entry won't fix it. The edge has to come from staying in winners and exiting losers, not from timing short bounces.

## Calm-regime leader 20-day-high swing, ATR bracket, vol-spike exit

**Training outcome:** 95 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 510688

**Lessons:** A selective 1-3 week swing entry that is idle 83% of the time cannot close the idle-capital gap. Its entries also showed no edge over simply holding the same stocks over the same periods. The universe's return comes from long, persistent uptrends in quality compounders. The next idea should stay invested much longer and rotate into those trends, for example relative-strength or momentum ranking with trend-following exits. Short mean-reversion or breakout swings that exit early do not suit this universe. Keep fee drag in check by trading less often per unit of time invested.

## Money-flow reclaim in a rising 50-SMA, ATR-bracketed 2-10 day swing

**Training outcome:** 81 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 17219

**Lessons:** Short fixed-horizon swing trades on daily NSE bars are dominated by fees and miss the trend compounding that drives holding returns. A negative edge_t of this size means tweaking parameters is unlikely to rescue the idea. A new idea should stay invested longer (trend-following or relative-strength rotation with trailing exits), trade less to cut the fee drag, and keep slots filled to close the idle-capital gap. It should also filter out structurally declining stocks.

## Rising-200-SMA 100-day breakout held on a 50-SMA close break

**Training outcome:** 149 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 12480075

**Lessons:** This swing/long-term rule suits beaten-down cyclicals and PSU names, not steady compounders. It needs a market-regime or drawdown guard (e.g., go flat when the index is below its long MA) to fix the 2011-style crash and the 40.9% dip. Slot contention is severe, so signals need ranking by strength rather than first-come entry. Low slot use against holding means idle capital costs about 0.46%/month.
