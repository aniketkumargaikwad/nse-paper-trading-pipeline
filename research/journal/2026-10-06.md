# Research notes — 2026-10-06

## Leader 20-day-high entry in rising 200-SMA, 12-bar time stop with ATR bracket

**Training outcome:** 84 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 412983

**Lessons:** A short-hold swing rule on daily bars fights the long uptrend in Indian quality stocks. It pays heavy fees and loses to buy-and-hold almost everywhere. The edge t-stat is significantly negative, so the problem is the idea itself, not noise. Gains appear only in broad trending years, which suggests a market regime filter matters more than the entry trigger. With only 2 versions left and no qualifying account, tweaking this idea is unlikely to reach 5-7% a month.

## Rising-200-SMA 100-day breakout held on a 100-SMA close break / 3-ATR chandelier

**Training outcome:** 161 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 17547471

**Lessons:** Trend-entry rules on daily bars sit out too much of strong compounders and still take big drawdowns in choppy years. The edge, if any, is concentrated in cyclical or high-volatility stocks; quality compounders should simply be held. A future idea needs drawdown control at the account level (a regime filter or portfolio stop) to get under the 30% dip limit, and better signal ranking given the 79% skip rate. Gains came from the market rising, not from skill, so to reach 5-7% a month the rule must stay invested much longer.
