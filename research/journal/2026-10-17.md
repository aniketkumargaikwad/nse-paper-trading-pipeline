# Research notes — 2026-10-17

## Leader 20-day volume breakout, 10-bar time stop with ATR bracket and trail

**Training outcome:** 90 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 707919

**Lessons:** Short swings of 10-15 days with frequent turnover cannot survive the fee load and miss the long trends where Indian equity returns are concentrated. Beating holding needs a rule that stays invested in strong, trending names, with fewer and longer trades. It also needs drawdown control at the account level, such as a market-regime filter, rather than per-trade timing. Per-stock gains on falling stocks do not add up to a qualifying account.

## Calm-tape 50-day leader breakout, vol-spike stress exit, fail-fast cull, ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 60 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 774554

**Lessons:** Rare, short (15-day) swing entries cannot beat holding on a universe dominated by long-term compounders. The idle-capital cost swamps any timing benefit. Exits are cut too early on winners like BAJFINANCE, TVSMOTOR and EICHERMOT. A viable rule needs much higher time-in-market: longer holds, trend-following exits instead of fixed short exits, and more frequent signals to fill 10 slots. It should also add an uptrend/quality filter to avoid repeatedly buying falling PSU and commodity names. Defensive value alone does not meet a 5-7% monthly target.
