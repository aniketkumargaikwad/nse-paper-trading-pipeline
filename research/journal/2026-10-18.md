# Research notes — 2026-10-18

## Uptrend RSI-14 dip reclaim, own-stock regime gate, ATR-bracketed 25-day swing

**Training outcome:** 90 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 340573

**Lessons:** Very short swing holds with low win rates get eaten by fees on NSE daily bars. Beating holding on falling stocks just means staying out, which is not an edge. A rule that wants 5-7% a month has to stay invested in trending strong stocks much longer and trade far less often. The fix is a trend or momentum hold with long holding periods and fewer round trips, not more tuning of short entries.
