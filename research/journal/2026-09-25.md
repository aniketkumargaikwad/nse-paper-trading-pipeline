## Unblocked 40-day high breakout in a rising 200-SMA regime, EMA trend exit

**Training outcome:** 519 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net -8785483

**Lessons:** Turnover was the dominant cost, not a parameter: fees exceeded gross profit, so any version of this entry rule loses before direction is considered. Slowing the clock only shrinks the loss, it never creates positive excess-vs-hold, which means the entry has no informational content. High signals_skipped_pct (68-79%) with only 10 slots means the account result is a near-random subsample of the basket, and sleeve_use_pct 0 on all accounts shows idle capital was never put to work, guaranteeing avg_excess_pct stays negative. Drawdowns of 43-100% on a 10-slot book point to no position-level risk cap and heavy correlated exposure to the same speculative names. A rule whose winners are all penny-volatility stocks and whose losers are all quality compounders is a mean-reversion bet fighting the market's drift.
