## 6-month leader EMA20 reclaim, held on a 50-SMA close break / 4-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 132 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 6308996

**Lessons:** A trend-following swing rule with about a 31% win rate makes money only in strong bull years and gives it back in sideways or bear years. That causes drawdowns above 30% and no edge over holding. Future ideas need a market-regime filter (index trend or breadth) that stands aside in 2010-11 and 2018-19 style markets. They also need tighter loss-cutting to stay under the 30% drawdown limit. Signals should be ranked by strength when slots are full instead of first-come, since 61% were skipped. Holding winners longer would capture compounders. Also fix the '_RiskOnly' object missing a 'name' attribute bug, which caused 2 simulation failures.
