# Research notes — 2026-09-19

## Daily leader breakout with a hard 13% stop that trails once green

**Training outcome:** 404 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 16539563

**Lessons:** Slot starvation, not signal quality, is the binding constraint: the day rule has the real edge (+1.49% active excess) but cannot fill 10 slots, while 60m fills slots but dilutes the edge to +0.48%. Raising frequency by dropping to faster timeframes destroys the edge - 5m is outright negative. The rule systematically loses to holding on structural compounders and only wins on mean-reverting laggards, which is why excess_vs_hold is so lopsided. With 1,177 combos tried, a t-stat of -1.27 on the sole qualifier is well inside selection noise; nothing here should be trusted out of sample. Any future idea needs to stay invested far more of the time or size fewer, better positions rather than chase trade count.
