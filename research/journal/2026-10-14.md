# Research notes — 2026-10-14

## 6-month leader 50-day breakout, held on a 100-SMA close break / 5-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 158 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 17020074

**Lessons:** Long ~100-day holds in a 10-slot account starve turnover and skip most signals. That makes it impossible to reach 10 trades a month and pushes month-to-month returns towards zero median. The idea suits cyclical and mean-reverting stocks, not secular compounders, where trading out loses massively. Future ideas need shorter holds or tighter exits to raise turnover, plus a universe or regime filter favouring cyclicals. The average must be judged with luck_check before trusting it.
