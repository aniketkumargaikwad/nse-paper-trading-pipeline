# Research notes — 2026-10-15

## Own-stock rising-200-SMA regime gate on 20-day thrust breakout, ATR bracket, 10-bar cap

**Training outcome:** 89 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 27771

**Lessons:** Short swing holds of about 10 days with a 40% win rate have almost no gross edge on NSE daily data once fees are paid. Each additional version of this idea has been churn. The money in this universe came from staying in long-running uptrends, and the idle-capital and missed-trend cost shows up in avg_excess_pct of -2.0. A new approach should hold winners for months, trade far less often so fees stop dominating, and include a market-regime or drawdown guard to keep the dip under 30%.

## ADX trend-birth thrust with money-flow confirmation, ATR-trailed 2-12 day swing

**Training outcome:** 96 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 327280

**Lessons:** Short swing trades of about 15 days, taken a few times per stock over 15 years, cannot keep up with a universe where holding returns 1.76% a month. The only real edge was avoiding drawdowns in losers. Version 3 is nowhere near the 5-7% monthly target, and another tweak will not close a 1.6%-per-month gap with negative edge_t. The next idea should stay invested much longer in leaders, for example relative-strength or trend rotation that holds the top-ranked stocks while their trend lasts. It should also trade less often to cut fees and keep slot use high.

## Leader 20-day volume breakout, held on a 2-close 50-SMA break / 3-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 149 of 194 combinations profitable after fees, net 15212584

**Lessons:** This is a trend-following entry with multi-week holds that suits volatile cyclicals and beaten-down names, but it gives up most of the upside on long-run compounders. With 10 slots, signal crowding (76% skipped) is the main problem: a ranking rule for which signal to take is needed. A market-regime filter and per-position risk caps are also needed to keep drawdown under 30% in years like 2010-2011. Raw profitability is not edge: active excess stayed negative.
