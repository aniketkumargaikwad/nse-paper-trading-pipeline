# Research notes — 2026-09-21

## Daily leader breakout with ATR stop and 1.5%-equity risk cap

**Training outcome:** 569 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 17731103

**Lessons:** Four versions in, the deficit is structural, not parametric: negative excess-active in the fully-funded basket means the entry signal has no informational content, so no amount of slot logic, sizing or timeframe retuning will reach 5-7%/month. Sub-hour bars are unusable at these fees (5m basket -2.27M net). Drawdowns above 100% prove the sizing/exit path can lose more than the position is worth - any future idea must cap per-trade risk and a hard stop before anything else is measured. Symbol selection matters more than the rule here: the same logic is +1000% excess on SUZLON and -25000% on BAJFINANCE, so a universe filter (realised vol / non-trending) belongs in the design, not as an afterthought.

## Risk-adjusted momentum leaders: 6-month gain > 10 daily ATRs, ATR swing exits

**Training outcome:** 280 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net -9493250

**Lessons:** Trade frequency is the dominant variable and it points the wrong way: 271 trades/month at 5m costs -4.26%/mo while 24 trades/month at 60m costs ~0. That means the entry signal carries no information beyond noise; only reducing its use improves results. A 5-7% monthly target cannot be reached by a rule whose gross edge is +0.4M against 9.9M of fees — future ideas need gross-per-trade large enough to clear costs by several multiples, which implies far longer holds (held_days on winners was 0.07-3.5 days) and far fewer, higher-conviction entries. Also: any rule that produces zero daily-timeframe trades over 15 years has a gate bug, not a strategy. edge_t is negative and significant everywhere — this is a real negative edge, not bad luck.
