# Research notes — 2026-09-22

## Daily 50-day leader breakout with a 2.2-ATR hard stop and half-off runner

**Training outcome:** 420 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net -857120

**Lessons:** Three versions have produced an account that never qualifies and loses to hold on active capital - the deficit is structural, not a parameter. The >100% drawdowns point to a sizing/leverage or short-exposure defect that must be fixed before any edge question is even askable. Fees exceeding gross PnL says trade frequency is the primary destroyer: 34k trades for -857k net. avg_excess_pct near -2% on every timeframe with slot_use 0.8-75% shows idle capital is also a permanent drag. A 5-7% monthly target is roughly 3x what holding these names delivered (1.93%/mo) - no version of this rule has shown anything resembling that.

## Uptrend washout reclaim, held on a Supertrend volatility trail (2-10 day swing)

**Training outcome:** 153 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net -89028148

**Lessons:** Frequency is the tell: when PnL degrades monotonically from day to 5m, the signal is noise and the only variable is cost. Check gross_pnl before fees first - if gross is negative, no amount of throttling, sizing or slot management saves it, and a further version of the same trigger is wasted. avg_excess_active_pct near zero on the best timeframe (day, -0.35%) means even the good case is just holding cash with extra steps. Also, 'beating hold' on collapsed stocks is a selection artifact; the bottom table shows the rule systematically destroys value on trending winners, so any future idea needs a trend filter that keeps it long in uptrends rather than flipping in and out. Target of 5-7%/month is ~30x away from this - incremental tuning cannot close it.

## Low-vol uptrend re-entry: EMA20 reclaim, 2.5-ATR hard stop, daily swing

**Training outcome:** 410 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net -5472048

**Lessons:** Judge on accounts, not baskets - the day basket's +8.9M evaporates to +0.65%/month once capital is shared across 10 slots with 76.6% of signals skipped. A positive gross PnL with fees exceeding it is not a near-miss; it means the per-trade edge is smaller than the round-trip cost, and no sizing or slot change fixes that. A 28% win rate needs a payoff ratio the exits clearly never delivered. Negative avg_excess_active_pct across four versions says the entries carry no information - the winners are just high-volatility names in an index that compounded ~400%, so beta is being mistaken for skill. Any successor must clear costs at a fraction of this turnover and must be measured against hold on the same names from the start.

## Full-occupancy daily uptrend rotation with scale-out and 3-ATR hard stop

**Training outcome:** 415 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net -4420467

**Lessons:** Three things are clear after five versions. First, fee drag is fatal: 28.1M of fees on 23.7M gross means the entry rule fires far too often for its edge; any future idea must clear costs per trade by design, not by filtering afterwards. Second, sub-daily timeframes are noise here - they scale monotonically worse as the bar shrinks, so intraday variants should not be revisited. Third, and most important, the account layer has a sizing bug: a 10-slot long-only book cannot fall 472-625% from a high. Those numbers indicate unbounded position sizing or compounding on notional rather than equity, and until that is fixed no account result is interpretable and no version can qualify. The basket numbers were never this catastrophic, which localises the fault to the account simulator, not the rule.
