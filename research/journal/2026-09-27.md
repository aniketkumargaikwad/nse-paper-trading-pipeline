# Research notes — 2026-09-27

## Rising-200-SMA 50-day breakout, 18-bar time stop, ATR bracket

**Training outcome:** 113 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 3663233

**Lessons:** Three versions have not produced a positive active edge, and the fall from a high is well beyond the limit. Swing exits of 15-27 days cut off long winners and only help on stocks in decline. Any next idea must stay invested in strong-trend names and add a market-regime filter to avoid years like 2011, 2015 and 2018. With 10 slots and 68% of signals skipped, how signals are ranked matters more than how many there are. A 5-7% average month is not reachable with this approach.

## Leader breakout held on a 50-day-average / 20% give-back trend exit

**Training outcome:** 146 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 14356511

**Lessons:** Trades lasting 40-80 days in 10 slots cannot reach 10 trades a month; the idea needs shorter holds or more slots to get to that frequency. The edge is concentrated in a few momentum bursts and is not statistically distinguishable from luck. The rule exits compounders far too early. A future idea should aim for higher turnover and for more consistent months, not rely on outlier years.
