# Research notes — 2026-09-24

## Quiet 120-day breakout, fail-fast EMA20 cull, half off at 3 ATR, EMA50 runner

**Training outcome:** 607 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 15256221

**Lessons:** Judge on avg_excess_active_pct first: a rule that loses to holding while its money is at work cannot be rescued by longer holds, wider stock lists, or slot tuning. Fees of 3.18M on 18.4M gross (17%) at 18,313 trades confirm the trade rate is far too high for the per-trade edge. A trigger that produces zero daily-bar trades over 187 months is mis-scaled, not selective. Positive net P&L across a 194-stock universe in a market where the average name returned 418% is meaningless without the hold benchmark. The idea's only signal of life is rare multi-week swing entries in high-beta cyclicals, which is a different rule, not a parameter tweak.

## Volatility-banded uptrend participation with a crash brake (daily-anchored swing)

**Training outcome:** 790 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 30752250

**Lessons:** Two versions have now confirmed the same shape: a long-only entry rule on NSE equities that is in the market a quarter of the time cannot beat a market whose average hold return is 400-1500%. The idle-capital gap (avg_excess_pct ~-1.5%/month) is the dominant term and no entry-signal tweak closes it; only a rule that stays invested materially longer can. Negative active excess on every timeframe says the entries themselves carry no information, so tuning thresholds, filters or stops is rearranging an edgeless signal. Any further work should start from staying invested by default and using the signal to size or rotate rather than to enter from cash, and should be judged on avg_excess_active_pct before returns.

## 60m regime-gated leader continuation with an ATR cost hurdle

**Training outcome:** 525 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 2200655

**Lessons:** Turnover is the binding constraint: at these costs any rule holding under a few days cannot pay its fees, so intraday variants of this family are not worth testing again. Beating hold requires either staying invested through trends or shorting; a long-only, 31%-win-rate, 30-day-hold rule does neither. A positive net_pnl across 1177 combos is meaningless when 93% of gross goes to fees and edge_t is negative everywhere. Account drawdowns 2-5x the basket drawdowns say the 10-slot concentration, not the signal, sets the risk; sizing must be set from the account curve, not per-symbol. 5-7% monthly is roughly 3x hold - no low-frequency long-only filter on this universe reaches it.

## Sticky uptrend: 20-day-high entry, 4.5-ATR give-back trail, instant re-entry

**Training outcome:** 924 of 1177 combinations profitable after fees, net 86906681

**Lessons:** Three structural things, in order of size. (1) The exit is fatally wrong for compounders - the entire bottom table is day-timeframe quality names (BAJFINANCE, ASTRAL, KEI, TRENT) where the rule made 300-2400% while holding made 5,000-26,000%; any rule that flattens on a pullback cannot be run on names in a multi-year uptrend, they need a trail that only exits on structural break or no exit at all. (2) Ten slots against 88% skipped signals is not a portfolio, it is a lottery, and it converts a 9-10% basket dip into a 33-54% account dip; slot allocation needs an explicit ranking and per-name risk sizing, not first-come. (3) Idle capital is the whole gap to hold - avg_excess_pct is ~-1%/month at every timeframe purely because the sleeve sits out; nothing closes that but staying invested longer. A 5-7% monthly account was never reachable from a rule whose best honest edge while invested is ~0.5%/month.
