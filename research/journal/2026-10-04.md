# Research notes — 2026-10-04

## Rising-200-SMA 100-day breakout, 9% target / 12-bar time stop swing

**Training outcome:** 93 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 376395

**Lessons:** Short swing exits in and out of strong compounders give up most of the trend: BAJFINANCE, ASTRAL, KEI and CHOLAFIN lost thousands of percent against holding. The edge is negative and statistically significant, not noise, so tuning parameters is unlikely to rescue it. Fee drag at roughly 40 trades a month kills thin edges. To approach 5%+ months, the rule must stay invested longer in the strongest stocks, hold winners and trade less. Mean-reversion or dip-buying in falling names is a defensive trait, not a source of return.
