**Lessons:** Intraday 0-3 DTE options trades entered in low IV leave nothing after charges. The only profitable pockets (mid IV rank, calm VIX) are too rare to build a strategy that trades often enough. Trend splits disagree between indexes (rising helps NIFTY and hurts BANKNIFTY), so they are noise. The next idea needs a gross edge per trade well above costs, about 0.15% or more. Position size must also fit the capital so sessions are not skipped. Monthly returns are nowhere near the 1.5-2% target, so tweaking this idea is unlikely to close the gap.

## Falling-VIX opening-range breakout call debit spread, intraday, 1% cap

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -4.79%/month, 6.4 trades/month, worst day -1.4%, luck t -4.79 - fails: fell 52.5% from a high (limit 35%); BANKNIFTY -5.98%/month, 5.0 trades/month, worst day -1.8%, luck t -4.56 - fails: fell 60.8% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** Most entries were in low IV rank, and nearly every trade lost about 0.5-1.5% intraday. That pattern suggests the structure pays away premium or time decay, or loses to costs, with no directional edge. A consistent loss with a high t-statistic in every regime means the core premise is wrong, not its tuning. Strike selection and capital sizing must be validated before backtesting: a 180% skip share and capital shortfalls make the results unreliable. The next idea should profit from low IV and sideways markets, the dominant regime here, with low turnover and cost-aware sizing.
