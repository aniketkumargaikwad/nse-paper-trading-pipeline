# Options research notes — 2026-11-20

## Overnight-held near-expiry iron condor, rolled every other session

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -1.15%/month, 2.0 trades/month, worst day -27.3%, luck t -1.88 - fails: worst day -27.3% (limit -10%); BANKNIFTY -2.64%/month, 0.8 trades/month, worst day -32.3%, luck t -3.93 - fails: worst day -32.3% (limit -10%)

**Lessons:** Selling or short-dated premium in low or normal IV, without a hard cap on loss per trade, produces losses of 25-30% in a day that no win rate can make up. Position size has to make the worst case survivable. Only use defined-risk structures, or size so that one bad day loses less than 10%. The only profitable bucket, high IV rank, is too rare: about 1.5 trades a year on NIFTY would fail the trade-frequency requirement. Any new idea needs a structural edge across ordinary days, not one small bucket.

## Event-flat trend bull call spread, 10% debit cap, exit before events

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.19%/month, 0.6 trades/month, worst day -10.4%, luck t -2.42 - fails: worst day -10.4% (limit -10%); BANKNIFTY +0.06%/month, 0.6 trades/month, worst day -9.4%, luck t -1.79 - fails: fell 39.4% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** An entry rule that fires about 7 times a year cannot reach 1.5-2% a month and leaves most months idle. Filters that look good inside one index's splits do not carry to the other, so filtering this idea further would be curve-fitting to small samples. Tail risk (-6% to -10% days) has to be capped by design, either with defined-risk structures or smaller size, before chasing returns. The next idea needs an entry that fires several times a month and has a reason to make money that holds in both indexes.

## Mid-VIX inside-open NIFTY/BANKNIFTY wide-wing iron condor, 1.2% cap

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.95%/month, 7.3 trades/month, worst day -2.0%, luck t -0.78 - fails: lost money on average: -0.95% a month; BANKNIFTY -5.37%/month, 4.1 trades/month, worst day -12.5%, luck t -3.23 - fails: worst day -12.5% (limit -10%)

**Lessons:** Do not sell or hold intraday option structures far from expiry on BANKNIFTY. Since late 2024 it has only monthly expiry, so strike and expiry logic must handle that or the index should be dropped. Most entries fell in low IV rank and normal VIX, where premium is cheap and this idea loses. Small positive buckets are not an edge. A strategy needs positive edge_t across the main regimes, not only in rare ones. Expiry-day (0-3 DTE) behaviour is the only area near neutral.

## Skew-rich daily-cycled bull put spread, VIX 13-30, 10% defined-risk budget

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.07%/month, 0.7 trades/month, worst day -6.1%, luck t -2.70 - fails: lost money on average: -0.07% a month; BANKNIFTY +0.06%/month, 0.7 trades/month, worst day -3.1%, luck t -2.00 - fails: too slow: 0.7 trades a month (needs 10, or 1.5% a month)

**Lessons:** Entry rules this selective cannot meet a 10-trades-a-month bar. Filtering further toward the profitable subsets (high VIX, falling trend, 0-3 DTE) would cut trades even more, and those subsets hold only 7-34 trades, so they are likely overfit. A high win rate with an unhedged tail is not an edge, so every short-premium position needs defined risk. A future idea should start from a rule that trades often by design, such as a systematic weekly defined-risk structure, and add filters only afterward. Sideways, low-IV regimes were the main source of losses.
