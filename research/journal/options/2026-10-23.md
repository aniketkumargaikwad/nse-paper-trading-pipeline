# Options research notes — 2026-10-23

## Trend-filtered weekly put credit spread sized to a 10% margin budget

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.24%/month, 0.6 trades/month, worst day -5.3%, luck t -3.05 - fails: fell 30.9% from a high (limit 30%); BANKNIFTY +0.03%/month, 0.6 trades/month, worst day -5.9%, luck t -2.05 - fails: too slow: 0.6 trades a month (needs 10, or 1.5% a month)

**Lessons:** A high win rate with negative edge_t means the tail losses dominate. Selling premium when IV rank is low and VIX is calm loses money. Trading 0.5 times a month cannot reach 1.5-2% a month even with a real edge, and adding trades to a negative-edge rule only adds losses. The next idea needs a frequent entry rule (10+ a month, or 1.5% a month) and must show a positive edge before any tuning. If premium is sold, it should be only in higher IV rank or VIX, with defined risk to cap -5% days.

## Wide-wing NIFTY intraday short straddle with hard 1.5% account cap

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -5.82%/month, 13.5 trades/month, worst day -2.4%, luck t -3.22 - fails: fell 57.9% from a high (limit 30%); BANKNIFTY -14.16%/month, 8.1 trades/month, worst day -5.0%, luck t -3.80 - fails: fell 90.1% from a high (limit 30%)

**Lessons:** This intraday structure has a reliably negative edge on both indexes, and charges make it worse. No market-condition filter rescues it, so tuning one parameter is very unlikely to move the average month from deeply negative to +1.5%. Position sizing and strike selection must fit the capital, or most sessions get skipped and the stats describe very few days. BANKNIFTY needs a strike and lot-size check, and the skip counting should be verified. The next idea should trade on most sessions, pay fewer charges per rupee of edge, and have a structural reason to win, such as selling premium with defined risk.

## Pullback-in-uptrend bull put spread, short hold, small defined risk

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.11%/month, 0.2 trades/month, worst day -8.2%, luck t -2.85 - fails: lost money on average: -0.11% a month; BANKNIFTY -0.15%/month, 0.2 trades/month, worst day -4.1%, luck t -2.32 - fails: lost money on average: -0.15% a month

**Lessons:** A filter this tight leaves too few trades to reach 1.5-2% a month, even if the trades were good. Premium-style entries at low IV rank in calm or sideways markets lose. Rich IV (high VIX or IV rank) is the only hint of an edge. A trade frequency of at least several per month is needed from the start. Negative edge_t on both indexes means tweaking one parameter will not rescue this idea.

## Afternoon theta-crush NIFTY iron condor (intraday, defined risk)

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -4.68%/month, 15.4 trades/month, worst day -1.7%, luck t -4.20 - fails: fell 49.6% from a high (limit 30%); BANKNIFTY -2.70%/month, 15.2 trades/month, worst day -1.7%, luck t -2.61 - fails: fell 32.1% from a high (limit 30%)

**Lessons:** The low-IV-rank intraday setup has a consistent negative edge on both indexes, and costs make it worse. Win rate does not matter when losers are bigger than winners. Before trusting any future result, fix the capital sizing and strike selection, because the skip share is above 100% of sessions. Also audit the skip counter for double counting. An idea that only shows promise at mid or high IV rank needs to actually trade in that regime, and that happens rarely in this period.
