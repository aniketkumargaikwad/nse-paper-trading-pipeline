# Options research notes — 2026-11-27

## Gap-down fill: intraday NIFTY/BANKNIFTY call debit spread on reclaimed open

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -3.49%/month, 3.4 trades/month, worst day -1.9%, luck t -3.24 - fails: fell 42.9% from a high (limit 35%); BANKNIFTY -5.83%/month, 3.7 trades/month, worst day -2.4%, luck t -4.48 - fails: fell 59.9% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** Almost every entry came at low IV rank, win rates were near zero and losses were consistent. That looks like intraday long premium, or stops that are too tight, being eroded by theta and charges. Total charges were about 18.7k on NIFTY and 33.8k on BANKNIFTY. No regime split is positive, so another tweak to this idea is unlikely to reach +1.5-2% a month. The BANKNIFTY strike selection does not fit the available chain and must be fixed in any next idea. A strongly negative edge suggests trying the opposite structure: defined-risk premium selling, such as iron condors or credit spreads near expiry, entered daily.

## Cheap-gamma long straddle: buy near-expiry ATM when IV trails realized range

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.50%/month, 3.4 trades/month, worst day -7.4%, luck t -2.07 - fails: fell 61.7% from a high (limit 35%); BANKNIFTY -0.32%/month, 4.0 trades/month, worst day -8.1%, luck t -1.71 - fails: fell 67.8% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** A negative edge_t on both indexes means the core idea loses money. Filtering by condition would leave only calm-VIX trades, which fixes neither the drawdown nor the target of a 1.5-2% average month. The next idea should use defined-risk structures and size each trade so a single day cannot cost 7-8%. It should be tested first for a positive edge in every regime, not just in one subset.

## Quiet-morning midday NIFTY short straddle: sell full-day IV, hold the calm afternoon

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -1.25%/month, 10.6 trades/month, worst day -1.3%, luck t -1.62 - fails: lost money on average: -1.25% a month; BANKNIFTY -3.09%/month, 10.5 trades/month, worst day -2.2%, luck t -4.12 - fails: fell 37.9% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** This intraday structure loses money in the conditions that make up 90%+ of sessions (normal or calm VIX, low IV rank), and charges of Rs 24-35k make the losses worse. A setup that only pays when IV is high cannot reach 10+ trades a month in a low-IV regime. BANKNIFTY with weekly-style strikes on monthly-only expiries breaks execution, so any new idea must use liquid near-expiry strikes or must stop assuming premium collection pays in low IV. The next idea should be one that works when IV rank is low and VIX is normal, since those are the conditions most days were traded in here.

## Trend-gated defined-risk synthetic long: weekly ATM bull call + bull put, 2.5% wings

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.11%/month, 0.5 trades/month, worst day -5.1%, luck t -2.79 - fails: lost money on average: -0.11% a month; BANKNIFTY -0.13%/month, 0.4 trades/month, worst day -5.6%, luck t -2.34 - fails: lost money on average: -0.13% a month

**Lessons:** The entry filter is so tight it fires a few times a year, and when it fires it mostly picks 4-7 DTE, low IV rank setups that lose. Low frequency alone rules out 1.5-2%/month regardless of trade quality. Selling or holding near-term premium at low IV rank in normal VIX has no edge here. The next idea needs at least several trades a month, entries that are not concentrated at low IV rank, and a hard cap on per-day loss.

**Lessons:** The position size does not fit the account, so most days fail the capital check. Trades on far expiries, especially BANKNIFTY at 16+ days, take heavy intraday losses. Low IV rank entries make up almost all trades, and all of them lose. Charges alone sink a strategy with a low win rate. The skip counter needs fixing so it counts sessions, not attempts. No earlier version can be confirmed as better from this report, and nothing from this idea should go to paper trading as it stands.
