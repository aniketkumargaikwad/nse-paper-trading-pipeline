# Options research notes — 2026-11-16

## VRP-gated defined-risk iron condor v4: loose VIX-vs-ATR gate that fires

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.34%/month, 0.9 trades/month, worst day -6.2%, luck t -3.30 - fails: lost money on average: -0.34% a month; BANKNIFTY -0.24%/month, 0.6 trades/month, worst day -5.9%, luck t -2.43 - fails: lost money on average: -0.24% a month

**Lessons:** Do not sell premium when IV rank is low or VIX is below 13. The edge, if there is one, only appears at mid or high IV, and filtering down to that leaves only a handful of trades a year, which fails the frequency rule. A high win rate with fat-tailed losses is a warning sign, not an edge. Any future premium-selling idea needs a built-in loss cap such as defined-risk spreads, plus an entry regime that occurs often enough to give at least 2-3 trades a month. Changing one thing in this version cannot turn a t of -3.3 into a t of +2.
