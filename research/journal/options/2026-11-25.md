# Options research notes — 2026-11-25

## Weekly broken-wing put butterfly: skew-funded dip payoff, rolled to expiry

**Training outcome:** NIFTY +0.62%/month, 1.2 trades/month, worst day -10.9%, luck t -0.47 - fails: worst day -10.9% (limit -10%); BANKNIFTY +0.59%/month, 1.3 trades/month, worst day -9.3%, luck t -0.85 - fails: too slow: 1.3 trades a month (needs 10, or 1.5% a month)

**Lessons:** A low-win-rate, rarely-triggering positional structure produces a lumpy, luck-driven P&L with a negative median. Filtering to the profitable buckets (top-IV third, falling trend) would leave about 5-9 trades in 10 years, which is overfit and even less frequent. A next idea needs many trades per month, a high win rate or a defined-risk structure, capped single-day loss under 10%, and a positive median month.

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.01%/month, 0.8 trades/month, worst day -3.8%, luck t -2.62 - fails: lost money on average: -0.01% a month; BANKNIFTY -0.06%/month, 0.8 trades/month, worst day -6.2%, luck t -2.17 - fails: lost money on average: -0.06% a month

**Lessons:** Two versions of this idea with a negative edge_t mean the core signal points the wrong way, not that it needs tuning. Very low trade frequency cannot reach 1.5-2% a month even with a real edge. The next idea needs a structural reason to make money, such as harvesting premium in high IV rank with defined risk or trend-following directional spreads, and must trade several times a month. VIX and trend splits that differ by index should not be used as filters without a reason that holds for both.

## Daily-cycled near-expiry ATM iron fly, 2% wings, 1-day hold, 8% margin

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -1.01%/month, 1.5 trades/month, worst day -22.8%, luck t -3.87 - fails: worst day -22.8% (limit -10%); BANKNIFTY -0.29%/month, 1.9 trades/month, worst day -16.3%, luck t -2.22 - fails: worst day -16.3% (limit -10%)

**Lessons:** Short-dated positional option trades without a hard per-day loss cap carry tail risk the limit cannot absorb. Any next idea needs defined-risk structures or stops sized so one day cannot exceed 10%. Avoid entries 4-7 days from expiry and in falling trends. Low IV rank dominated the trade count and lost money. A win rate near 50% with negative average P&L means the losers are too large. Trade frequency of about 1.5 a month is too low to reach a 1.5-2% average month.
