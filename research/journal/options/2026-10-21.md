# Options research notes — 2026-10-21

## Late-day momentum call debit spread v2 (exit mechanics fixed)

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -5.53%/month, 6.7 trades/month, worst day -2.0%, luck t -2.87 - fails: fell 55.7% from a high (limit 30%); BANKNIFTY -7.01%/month, 5.3 trades/month, worst day -2.6%, luck t -3.88 - fails: fell 64.4% from a high (limit 30%)

**Lessons:** Every split loses: every VIX bucket, every trend, every expiry bucket, every IV rank. So this is not a filtering problem; the core entry has negative edge. Win rates of 11-22% with steady small losses suggest a long-premium setup bleeding theta and hitting stops in mostly low-IV, sideways sessions (most trades). Low IV rank dominated and was a poor time for this idea. A strongly negative edge_t on both indexes means this idea should be dropped rather than tuned.

## Trend-following ITM-to-OTM bull call debit spread (positional, monthly)

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.04%/month, 0.4 trades/month, worst day -7.1%, luck t -2.77 - fails: lost money on average: -0.04% a month; BANKNIFTY +0.23%/month, 0.2 trades/month, worst day -7.7%, luck t -1.73 - fails: too slow: 0.2 trades a month (needs 10, or 1.5% a month)

**Lessons:** Three versions of this positional idea have stayed at well under one trade a month with no statistical edge, so it cannot reach 1.5-2% a month. Filters that help one index hurt the other, so condition-slicing is overfitting. A viable idea has to trade often (near-weekly or more, closer to expiry), have a defined maximum loss per trade to cap 5-7% days, and show a positive edge_t on both indexes before fine-tuning.

## Quiet-open intraday iron fly with VIX-falling filter and hard daily cap

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -7.11%/month, 2.1 trades/month, worst day -23.3%, luck t -3.46 - fails: worst day -23.3% (limit -10%); BANKNIFTY -7.84%/month, 0.9 trades/month, worst day -27.3%, luck t -2.96 - fails: worst day -27.3% (limit -10%)

**Lessons:** A strongly negative edge_t on both indexes means the core entry logic is wrong, not the parameters. The losses are large single-day blowups (-23% to -27%), which points to missing per-trade loss caps and over-sized positions relative to capital. Low IV rank and calm VIX entries lost consistently, so this setup should not trade in low-volatility regimes. Strike selection fails often on BANKNIFTY, and charges are a large drag at this trade frequency. Further tweaks to this idea are not worth the last version.

## Post-opening-range intraday iron fly, 1.5% wings, hard 1.2% cap

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -15.92%/month, 10.6 trades/month, worst day -6.7%, luck t -8.74 - fails: fell 91.5% from a high (limit 30%); BANKNIFTY -21.23%/month, 6.0 trades/month, worst day -13.4%, luck t -3.48 - fails: worst day -13.4% (limit -10%)

**Lessons:** A 3-5% win rate with losses in every regime means the entry has no edge, probably buying premium that decays intraday. Charges (62.9k and 83.1k) were large next to the capital. Skip counts above 100% of sessions mean position size is far beyond the account. The next idea should fit the capital first and should not depend on intraday long premium. I could not compare with earlier versions because their results were not in this input.
