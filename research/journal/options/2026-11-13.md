# Options research notes — 2026-11-13

## Post-opening-range NIFTY intraday short straddle on calm-VIX inside days

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.51%/month, 12.7 trades/month, worst day -1.7%, luck t -0.59 - fails: lost money on average: -0.51% a month; BANKNIFTY -1.56%/month, 11.3 trades/month, worst day -2.9%, luck t -1.62 - fails: lost money on average: -1.56% a month

**Lessons:** Size positions to the available capital, or use defined-risk structures, so the strategy can actually trade most sessions. Pick liquid strikes so legs fill. Avoid selling premium when IV rank is low. Mid-week entries (4-7 days to expiry) were the worst bucket. Charges eat thin intraday edges, so a new idea needs a clearly larger per-trade edge or fewer, higher-quality trades. Never judge on splits with fewer than 20 trades.

## Always-on weekly ATM bull put spread: equity drift plus put skew premium

**Training outcome:** NIFTY -0.41%/month, 1.0 trades/month, worst day -6.0%, luck t -3.56 - fails: fell 58.5% from a high (limit 35%); BANKNIFTY -0.28%/month, 1.2 trades/month, worst day -9.7%, luck t -2.47 - fails: fell 50.7% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** Selling premium in normal or low IV is negatively paid. The tail losses exceed the collected premium, and a high win rate hides this. Restricting entries to VIX above 18 would leave about 0.3 trades a month, too infrequent to reach 1.5-2% a month or to pass the frequency expectation. Filtering this idea down cannot both fix the drawdown and keep enough trades. Any short-volatility idea needs defined risk (spreads) and IV-conditioned entry from the start, and should avoid entries very close to expiry.

## Fear-open fade: 09:20 NIFTY short straddle only when VIX opens up, 1.5% cap

**Training outcome:** NIFTY +0.05%/month, 2.2 trades/month, worst day -1.6%, luck t -0.16 - fails: too slow: 2.2 trades a month (needs 10, or 1.5% a month); BANKNIFTY -0.22%/month, 2.1 trades/month, worst day -3.3%, luck t -0.84 - fails: lost money on average: -0.22% a month

**Lessons:** The entry rule fires too rarely, and filtering it toward the profitable middle-IV slice would make it rarer still, so the frequency rule cannot be met by tuning it. The idea loses in low-IV, sideways, calm conditions, which are the most common. A new idea should trade most sessions by design, around 10 or more times a month, for example a daily near-expiry structure. It should also avoid relying on a premium edge when IV is low and on BANKNIFTY far-dated expiries.

## Always-on bull call debit spread, 7% premium cap, VIX outside 13-18 only

**Training outcome:** NIFTY +1.85%/month, 3.3 trades/month, worst day -18.0%, luck t +0.88 - fails: worst day -18.0% (limit -10%); BANKNIFTY +0.17%/month, 3.6 trades/month, worst day -12.7%, luck t -1.22 - fails: worst day -12.7% (limit -10%)

**Lessons:** A headline average built on one outlier year is not an edge; check edge_t and the median month first. Limit entries to low IV rank and 4-7 DTE, and avoid mid and high IV rank and 0-3 DTE on BANKNIFTY. Tail risk needs hard per-day loss caps or defined-risk structures, because event days (Covid, election results) breach the -10% limit. Results did not carry from NIFTY to BANKNIFTY, so the idea is not robust.
