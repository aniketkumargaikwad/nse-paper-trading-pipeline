# Research notes — 2026-10-12

## 12-1 momentum leader EMA20 reclaim, held on a 50-SMA break / 3-ATR chandelier

**Training outcome:** 132 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 8039921

**Lessons:** This swing-trend entry (about 13-day holds, 34% win rate) only works in strong trends. It gives back gains in sideways years and cuts winners far too early on compounding growth stocks (BAJFINANCE, ASTRAL, KEI), so it cannot beat buy-and-hold. 77% of signals are skipped, so signal ranking matters. A good per-stock average does not mean an account edge: drawdown and negative edge_t sink it. Further tweaks are unlikely to lift a ~1% month with negative edge to 5-7%.

## Strong-trend 55-day breakout, 12-bar time stop, 2.5-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 89 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 506180

**Lessons:** Short swing entries on daily bars give no robust edge, and fee drag is severe. Results depend on the market regime, so the next idea needs a regime or trend filter that stays out in choppy years. It also needs a hard cap on account drawdown to meet the 30% limit. Stay invested longer in strong uptrends to close the idle-capital gap. Rank competing signals instead of taking them first-come.
