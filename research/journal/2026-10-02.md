# Research notes — 2026-10-02

## 100-day breakout with own-stock 200-SMA regime gate (index filter not expressible)

**Training outcome:** 150 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 11970891

**Lessons:** After three versions the rule still shows no real edge over holding. Its gains come from avoiding losers, not from catching winners. Bull-market years carry the result, and there is no protection in market-wide selloffs like 2011. With 74% of signals skipped, a next idea needs a way to rank signals, such as relative strength. It also needs a market-level filter to keep drawdowns under 30%, and it should stay in trending leaders longer instead of exiting them early.

## 250-day high breakout in rising 200-SMA, ATR bracket, 15-day time stop

**Training outcome:** 112 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 3312017

**Lessons:** Short fixed-length swing holds give up compounding on trending winners and cannot close the idle-capital gap. Account drawdown needs a regime or market filter to avoid 2011/2015-16-style years. The idea suits mean-reverting or cyclical stocks, not quality compounders. The slot scarcity, with 59% of signals skipped, means signal ranking matters. A negative edge_t means the result should not be trusted out of sample.
