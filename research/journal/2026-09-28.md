# Research notes — 2026-09-28

## Quiet-compounder EMA20 reclaim, held until 200-SMA break or 6-ATR giveback

**Training outcome:** 96 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 5959007

**Lessons:** On NSE daily data 2010-2025, buy-and-hold on quality compounders sets a very high bar (1.76% a month). A rule that exits early or avoids persistent uptrends gives most of that up. Swing exits with a win rate under 40% bleed money. Future ideas should stay invested in strong, persistent uptrends: relative-strength ranking to choose among the many skipped signals, long holds, and trailing exits rather than tight targets. Judge on active excess and edge_t, not on the top/bottom tables.

**Lessons:** Short fixed-horizon swing entries on daily NSE bars cannot overcome costs; the per-trade edge is too small relative to fees. Holding stocks returned about 1.76% a month, so any rule must stay invested longer and ride trends, not scalp them. Low slot use kills account returns. Next idea should be a trend/momentum hold: fewer, longer trades that stay in winners, with a market-regime filter to cap drawdown under 30%.

## 250-day high breakout in rising 200-SMA, 2-ATR initial stop, 4-ATR / 50-SMA trail

**Training outcome:** 137 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 10070621

**Lessons:** This is a slow, long-hold swing/trend rule. It works in trending regimes and on volatile or cyclical stocks, and loses heavily by exiting multi-bagger compounders early. Ten slots with holds of 35 to 75 days saturate the account, cap trade frequency below the 10-per-month bar and skip most signals. To qualify, the next idea needs either shorter holds with faster turnover to reach at least 10 trades a month, or a far larger return per month. Signal ranking matters when 70% of signals are skipped. A regime filter could avoid years like 2011, 2019 and 2025. A ~1.6% average month is far from the 5-7% target.
