# Rotation research notes — 2026-11-12

## 12-1 momentum, 20 stocks, slower NIFTY 300-day cash filter

**Training outcome:** +2.30%/yr vs the paper rule (t 1.01), worst fall 28.7%, beat NIFTY in 82% of years - fails: its lead over the paper-traded rule could be luck: t = 1.01 (needs 1.65)

**Lessons:** Tuning the market-filter length is a weak lever: it shifts when we are in cash, not which stocks we hold, and trades drawdown for noisy gains. Small lookback tweaks like 200 vs 300 days risk overfitting and will not clear t >= 1.65 against the baseline. Expect a real edge from the stock-selection side, not from filter timing.

## 12-1 momentum, 20 stocks, own 200-day trend gate, NIFTY 200-day filter

**Training outcome:** +0.38%/yr vs the paper rule (t 0.44), worst fall 22.0%, beat NIFTY in 88% of years - fails: its lead over the paper-traded rule could be luck: t = 0.44 (needs 1.65)

**Lessons:** Trend filters on single stocks mostly duplicate what 12-1 momentum and the market-level 200-day filter already do, so they are not a source of independent edge here. Changes to filters on the same ranking are unlikely to reach t > 1.65 against this baseline. To get a separable edge, change how stocks are ranked (risk-adjusted or residual momentum) instead of adding more trend gates.

## 12-0 momentum, 20 stocks, NIFTY 200-day filter (isolation re-test)

**Training outcome:** +2.82%/yr vs the paper rule (t 2.04), worst fall 21.9%, beat NIFTY in 88% of years - PASSES

**Lessons:** The variant mainly amplifies strong trending years and gives back some ground in choppy or reversal years such as 2020-21. A 2.8% lead with 53% of years ahead and t near 2, after several tries, should be treated as possibly noise rather than a proven edge. Using the single remaining version to tune further would raise overfitting risk more than it would add information. If v4 is adopted, paper-trade it alongside the baseline and expect returns close to the baseline's.
