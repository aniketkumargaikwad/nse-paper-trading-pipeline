# Rotation research notes — 2026-10-22

## 12-1 momentum, 20 stocks, 50-day own-trend confirmation, NIFTY 200-day filter

**Training outcome:** -0.57%/yr vs the paper rule (t -0.36), worst fall 21.9%, beat NIFTY in 82% of years - fails: no better than the momentum rule already paper-traded (-0.57% a year against it)

**Lessons:** A per-stock short-trend filter on top of 12-1 momentum is mostly redundant, because top-momentum stocks are already above their trends. Its main effect is extra turnover and early exits in trending markets. Making the filter slower would mostly bring the result back to the baseline rather than beat it. Any real improvement probably has to come from a different dimension, such as ranking quality, volatility-adjusted momentum, or position sizing, not from more exit filters.

## 12-1 momentum, 20 stocks, exclude crash-prone names (vol cap 50%), NIFTY 200d

**Training outcome:** -5.32%/yr vs the paper rule (t -2.83), worst fall 23.2%, beat NIFTY in 76% of years - fails: no better than the momentum rule already paper-traded (-5.32% a year against it)

**Lessons:** In this universe, momentum's edge comes from the high-volatility, crash-prone names, so filtering on crash risk cuts the right tail without trimming the left tail. Drawdown is already set by the market-level 200-day filter, so stock-level risk screens don't move worst fall. Future ideas should either add return (better ranking or timing) or attack drawdown at the portfolio or regime level, not exclude stocks.

## 6-1 momentum, 20 stocks, NIFTY 200-day filter (shorter formation)

**Training outcome:** -1.25%/yr vs the paper rule (t -0.27), worst fall 22.4%, beat NIFTY in 71% of years - fails: no better than the momentum rule already paper-traded (-1.25% a year against it)

**Lessons:** A shorter lookback adds turnover and cost without adding edge. Its wins cluster in sharp-recovery years, which looks like timing luck. The 12-1 signal with the 200-day filter is hard to beat by retuning the lookback. Cutting costs and turnover, or adding an independent signal, is more promising than changing the horizon.

## 12-1 momentum, 20 stocks, faster NIFTY 100-day cash filter

**Training outcome:** -0.57%/yr vs the paper rule (t -0.27), worst fall 21.8%, beat NIFTY in 82% of years - fails: no better than the momentum rule already paper-traded (-0.57% a year against it)

**Lessons:** Making the market-trend filter faster just trades V-recovery gains for whipsaw losses in trending years, so the net is about zero. The baseline's 200-day filter is not the bottleneck. Small drawdown improvements of 1-2 points are within noise and do not justify replacing a rule already being paper-traded. With one version left, tweaking filter speed again is unlikely to produce a significant lead.
