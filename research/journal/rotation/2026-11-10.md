# Rotation research notes — 2026-11-10

## Risk-adjusted 12-1 momentum, 20 stocks, NIFTY 200-day filter

**Training outcome:** -2.39%/yr vs the paper rule (t -1.63), worst fall 19.9%, beat NIFTY in 76% of years - fails: no better than the momentum rule already paper-traded (-2.39% a year against it)

**Lessons:** Vol-adjusting 12-1 momentum on NSE gives up return for only small drawdown gains. It also lost every year since 2016, which suggests the old edge has faded. The baseline's 200-day filter already supplies most of the downside protection, so further risk scaling of the ranking adds little. Three versions of this idea have not beaten the baseline; drop it.

## 12-0 momentum, 20 stocks, own 200-day trend gate, NIFTY 200-day filter

**Training outcome:** +2.55%/yr vs the paper rule (t 1.76), worst fall 21.9%, beat NIFTY in 82% of years - PASSES

**Lessons:** A stock-level trend filter on top of 12-1 momentum and the NIFTY filter gives a small, uneven edge. It comes from a few trending years, not a steady improvement. Using the last version today to tune the filter length or add a buffer would mostly fit noise. The baseline stays the honest default. v4 is a candidate worth re-testing on out-of-sample or paper-trading data, but it is not a proven upgrade. Higher costs and a worse worst day should be watched.
