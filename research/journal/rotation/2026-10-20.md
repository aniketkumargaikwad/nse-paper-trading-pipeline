# Rotation research notes — 2026-10-20

## Raw 12-1 momentum, top 20, inverse-volatility sizing, NIFTY 200-day filter

**Training outcome:** -2.28%/yr vs the paper rule (t -2.26), worst fall 21.2%, beat NIFTY in 76% of years - fails: no better than the momentum rule already paper-traded (-2.28% a year against it)

**Lessons:** Within a 20-stock momentum basket, volatility is part of the return source, so down-weighting it gives up return for only a slight cut in drawdown. Weighting tweaks inside the same selection move results by a couple of percent and tend to cost more than they save. A bigger difference is more likely to come from what is selected or from when the strategy goes to cash than from how positions are sized. The baseline remains the version to beat.

## Concentrated 12-1 momentum, 12 stocks, own-trend confirmation

**Training outcome:** +2.93%/yr vs the paper rule (t 1.65), worst fall 23.7%, beat NIFTY in 76% of years - fails: its lead over the paper-traded rule could be luck: t = 1.65 (needs 2)

**Lessons:** Fewer stocks is mostly a variance dial on the same signal. It adds return in a few trend years and does not improve consistency or drawdown. Testing more position counts (10, 15) would be searching for a lucky parameter and undermines the pre-registered test. The paper-traded 20-stock baseline stays the rule, and later ideas should add independent information rather than retune holdings count.
