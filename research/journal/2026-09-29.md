# Research notes — 2026-09-29

## 250-day high breakout in rising 200-SMA, held on 100-SMA break or 5-ATR chandelier

**Training outcome:** 158 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 23489210

**Lessons:** Slow long-term signals with holds of about 60-150 days cannot reach 10 trades a month on a 10-slot account. The high average comes from one or two outlier years, and the median month is zero. The rule gets out of strong compounders too early. To qualify, a future idea needs much higher signal frequency, or a way to stay invested in trending names instead of exiting. Judge any idea on its median month and edge_t, not its mean.
