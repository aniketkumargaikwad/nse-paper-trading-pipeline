# Research notes — 2026-10-07

## Leader MACD histogram re-acceleration, held on a 50-SMA break / 4-ATR trail

**Training outcome:** 124 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 7505691

**Lessons:** A 20-45 day long-only entry on daily bars mostly rides market beta and gives it back in bear years. Without a market-regime filter, drawdown breaches the limit. Being profitable is not the same as having an edge: active excess is negative. With 71% of signals skipped, the next idea needs a ranking among competing signals, a regime gate (index above its long average) and longer holds on winners. Even a qualifying tweak of this rule is unlikely to get from 1.2% to the 5% aim.

## Leader 100-day breakout with own-stock regime gate and regime-off exit (v3)

**Training outcome:** 147 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 8873611

**Lessons:** This rule acts as a downtrend filter: it adds value on decaying stocks but exits compounders far too early (BAJFINANCE, ASTRAL, TRENT). Account drawdown came from crowded entries in bear years such as 2011, so a market-regime filter or a per-trade risk cap is needed to stay under 30%. The high skip rate means signal ranking matters. Since excess is negative both overall and while active, the idea lacks real edge and would need longer holds or trend-strength ranking to approach 5%/month.
