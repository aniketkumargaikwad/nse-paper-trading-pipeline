# Research notes — 2026-10-09

## Calm-tape 100-day leader breakout, own-stock volatility regime gate, 50-SMA hold

**Training outcome:** 106 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 2442681

**Lessons:** Avoiding losers is not an edge when the universe is dominated by long-run compounders. The rule needs to stay invested longer and ride trends, not take short entries that exit early. Low slot use combined with a high skip rate means entries arrive in bursts; a different idea should give steadier exposure, for example persistent trend or momentum holding with ranking. Three versions have not moved active excess above zero, so tweaking this idea further is unlikely to reach 5-7%.

## Own-stock regime-gated 55-day leader breakout, 50-SMA trend-break exit

**Training outcome:** 139 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 11092552

**Lessons:** A ten-slot account with no market-regime filter takes a >30% drawdown in bear years like 2011; a broad-index trend filter or per-trade stop is needed. Short holds of ~40-60 days give up the long compounding of quality stocks, so excess vs hold stays negative. With 74% of signals skipped, signal ranking matters as much as entry. Positive active excess that is not statistically meaningful is not an edge.
