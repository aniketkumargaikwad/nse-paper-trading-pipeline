# Index allocation research notes — 2026-11-24

## v1.1 Midcap150 Momentum 50 + NIFTY500 Momentum 50, each on own 100-day average, vol target 30%

**Training outcome:** 1.65%/month (20.1%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 27.0%, luck t 3.26, invested 68% of the time - PASSES

**Lessons:** Midcap150 Momentum 50 / Nifty500 Momentum rotation with a cash filter is robust (v1 passes all checks, nearby 7/7). The fall is controlled by the cash exit, so leave it alone. Return is lost in sideways years (2013, 2016, 2018-19, 2022, 2025) through whipsaw and late re-entry, not in crashes. The next lever is switch hysteresis or confirmation to cut switching below about 4/yr. Do not loosen the cash filter to raise invested_pct; that risks bringing back 2008-type falls. Watch whether alpha keeps decaying after 2019. Keep v1 as the baseline.

## v1.2 Mid+500 Momentum, own 100-day avg, vol target 30% on slower 120-session window

**Training outcome:** 1.63%/month (19.7%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 29.6%, luck t 3.15, invested 68% of the time - fails: no better than 'Midcap150 Momentum 50, vol target 30% + own 100-day average, weekly' (22.4% a year, worst fall 27.1%)

**Lessons:** Widening from Midcap150 to a 500-stock universe with the same own-100-day plus vol-target overlay does not help: it is lower CAGR and a deeper fall than the Midcap150 version. Do not retry the 500 universe with this overlay. The weak spot is choppy years such as 2013, 2016, 2018, 2019, 2022 and 2025. Being 68% invested with about 6 switches a year bleeds return and costs there. Future tries should target whipsaw, for example with a hysteresis band or a slower filter, not crash protection, which already works. The Midcap150 vol-30% plus 100-day plan remains the bar at 22.4% a year with a 27.1% fall.

## v2.1 Midcap150 Momentum 50, vol target 25% + own 100-day average, weekly

**Training outcome:** 1.77%/month (21.9%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 22.0%, luck t 3.94, invested 67% of the time - PASSES

**Lessons:** The vol target moved the fall most: it cut exposure before the 2008 and 2011 crashes. Return was lost in sideways or narrow years (2013, 2018, 2019, 2025), where momentum lagged and partial cash missed the benchmark's grind higher. Alpha is decaying in recent years, so watch out-of-sample performance before trusting the 2020s. Adding more filters to fix choppy years is not worth trying: it risks overfitting a plan that already passes and is robust to nearby settings. A new idea should target the choppy-year gap, not more crash protection.
