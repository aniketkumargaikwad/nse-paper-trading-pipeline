# Index allocation research notes — 2026-11-26

## v1.1 Midcap150 Momentum 50, falling back to Alpha Low-Vol 30, own 100-day, vol 30%

**Training outcome:** 1.84%/month (22.7%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 27.1%, luck t 3.96, invested 75% of the time - PASSES

**Lessons:** v1 is a valid fallback: it passes every check. The fall is controlled by the cash fallback in big crashes, so do not loosen it. Return is lost in choppy, range-bound years (2018, 2019, 2022, 2025), where repeated switching and momentum reversals cost money and add fees. Alpha is decaying, so judge any change mainly on the second half and the recent years. Do not chase extra bull-year gains: they are already large. If a change does not lift second-half alpha without raising the worst fall above about 30%, keep v1.

## v1.2 Midcap150 Mom 50 / Alpha LowVol 30 fallback, own 100-day, vol 30%, monthly dwell

**Training outcome:** 1.67%/month (20.1%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 38.2%, luck t 3.13, invested 74% of the time - fails: fell 38.2% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** The fall is driven by how fast the switch reacts, not by which assets it holds. A slow (monthly-style) switch let a -25% month through. Weekly 100-day-average exits plus vol targeting are what kept the reference plan's fall at 27%. The alpha/lowvol fallback cost return in sideways or rotation years (2018-2020, 2022, 2025). Alpha decays in the second half, so do not rely on the fallback leg for return. Not worth trying again: monthly or slow regime switches on midcap momentum. Keep anything new that does not beat 22.5%/27.1% from being sent through.

## v1.3 Midcap150 Momentum 50, vol target 30% on 40-session window + own 100-day, weekly

**Training outcome:** 1.81%/month (22.3%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 27.6%, luck t 3.89, invested 68% of the time - fails: no better than 'Midcap150 Momentum 50, vol target 30% + own 100-day average, weekly' (22.5% a year, worst fall 27.1%)

**Lessons:** Midcap150 Momentum 50 with a 30% vol target and a trend filter is a plateau at about 22.3-22.5% CAGR with a worst fall around 27%. Small filter or lookback tweaks only trade a tenth of a percent of return against fall, so don't keep tuning this family. The fall comes from 2008 and the 2018-2019 midcap crash. The return lost comes from flat or rotating years (2013, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2025), where momentum lags and cash drags. Alpha is shrinking in the second half (8.9 vs 14.6). To beat the incumbent, try a structurally different idea, such as a fallback to a defensive or low-vol index instead of cash when the filter is off, or blending momentum with quality, rather than another filter variant.
