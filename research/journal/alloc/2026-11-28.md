# Index allocation research notes — 2026-11-28

## v1.1 Midcap150 Momentum 50, falling back to Alpha Low-Vol 30, then FMCG, own 100-day, vol 30%

**Training outcome:** 1.69%/month (20.3%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 37.7%, luck t 3.15, invested 82% of the time - fails: fell 37.7% from a high (limit 35%)

**Lessons:** Momentum-50 with a simple fall-back switch still lets through a fall of about 38%. It switches 6.8 times a year, which is late in fast crashes (2008, the worst month of -17.9%) and whipsaws in choppy midcap years (2018-19, 2025), where it lags the benchmark by 15-18 points. Costs were large (1.84M). The best known version is vol target 30% plus own 100-day average, weekly (22.5%, fall 27.1%). Vol targeting is what cuts the fall, so fall-back-only variants of this idea are not worth trying again. A new idea needs vol scaling or a different signal to beat 27.1% and 22.5%.

## v2.1 Midcap Momentum 50 + Alpha Low-Vol 30 half each, Midcap Quality backup

**Training outcome:** 1.58%/month (19.4%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 26.2%, luck t 3.71, invested 72% of the time - fails: no better than 'Midcap150 Momentum 50, vol target 25% + own 100-day average, weekly' (21.9% a year, worst fall 22.0%)

**Lessons:** Blending the low-vol 30 sleeve at half weight cut return without making the fall shallower than the incumbent's own vol target plus 100-day average. Diluting momentum with low-vol is not worth retrying. The cost of this plan is sideways years (2013, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2025), where trend exits whipsaw and cash lags the benchmark. Costs were about 1.07M, which is significant at 6.8 switches a year. To beat 21.9% / 22.0%, a plan must cut whipsaw in flat years. Possible routes are slower exits, fewer switches, or staying partly invested, rather than adding defensive sleeves. Also watch the decaying second-half alpha.

## v3.1 Midcap150 Momentum 50, Alpha Low-Vol 30 fallback, own 100-day, vol target 25%

**Training outcome:** 1.79%/month (22.1%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 23.0%, luck t 3.98, invested 73% of the time - PASSES

**Lessons:** The trend/cash filter is what moved the fall: it cut 2008 and 2011 losses sharply. It is also what cost return in slow, choppy uptrends (2018-19, 2022, 2025), with only 73% invested. Second-half alpha is weaker, so expect lower alpha going forward. Do not keep tuning filter thresholds to rescue recent years; that is curve-fitting on a plan that already passed. Paper-trade v3 next. A new idea worth trying would be lowering turnover or costs, for example a slower rebalance, rather than changing the signals.
