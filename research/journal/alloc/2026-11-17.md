# Index allocation research notes — 2026-11-17

## v1.1 Midcap Momentum or Large-cap Quality, whichever leads, trend and vol guarded

**Training outcome:** 1.28%/month (14.7%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 35.3%, luck t 1.62, invested 81% of the time - fails: averaged 1.28% a month (the target is 1.5%)

**Lessons:** Midcap-momentum versus large-cap-quality rotation pays in strong trending bull years. It whipsaws in choppy or narrow markets (2016, 2018-19, 2022, 2025) and gives up 10-25 points against the benchmark in those years. Switching about 6 times a year burns about 16% of the end value in costs. The alpha is concentrated before 2016, so the t-stat stays weak. If hysteresis or a slower signal does not fix the post-2016 years, drop this idea rather than tune it further. Cash (81% invested) was not the main drag; wrong-side switches were.

## v1.2 Midcap Momentum or Large-cap Quality by 6+12m strength, trend and vol guarded

**Training outcome:** 1.23%/month (14.0%/yr; NIFTY 50 12.2%), worst fall 35.9%, luck t 1.43, invested 82% of the time - fails: averaged 1.23% a month (the target is 1.5%)

**Lessons:** The midcap-momentum vs large-cap-quality switch whipsaws in sideways or midcap-underperforming years (2016, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2025) and keeps losing money while the bench rises. Its edge is concentrated in 2007-2017 and decays afterwards, so t-stat is nowhere near 2.6 and one tweak will not double it. The 2011 and 2008 drawdowns show the regime filter reacts too slowly. High switch frequency (about 6 a year) costs about 11% of end value. Do not keep iterating on this two-sleeve switch. Prefer ideas with steadier, lower-turnover alpha and a faster crash exit.
