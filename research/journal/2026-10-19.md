# Research notes — 2026-10-19

## Leader EMA20 tag-and-go, 15-day time stop with 8% target

**Training outcome:** 117 of 200 combinations profitable after fees, net 2089566

**Lessons:** Swing entries held about 15 days give up most of the trend in compounding stocks: the bottom table is full of 50-300x winners where the rule made a fraction of holding. Frequent round trips with high fees destroy the thin gross edge. When idle capital is held, the owner pays roughly 1.4% a month in avg_excess. A version worth pursuing needs either much longer holds that ride trends, so the money is invested long enough to capture holding's return, or a regime filter that cuts drawdowns below 30%. A single tweak to this swing idea is unlikely to turn an edge_t of -5.3 positive.

## Calm-regime leader 3-down-day reclaim, ATR-bracketed 2-10 day swing

**Training outcome:** 85 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 107210

**Lessons:** Short swing holds of about 15 days, at about 3.7 trades a month across 194 stocks, leave capital idle 85% of the time and pay heavy fees for no active edge. A 5-7% average month cannot come from 15% slot use. The next idea must stay invested much longer and trade less: ride persistent trends or relative strength in the stocks that compound, and keep the fee drag small. Also fix the _RiskOnly.name crash.

## 52-week-high leader 3-day dip reclaim, ADX-gated, fail-fast then EMA50-ATR ride

**Training outcome:** 109 of 198 combinations profitable after fees, net 3102541

**Lessons:** The rule is mainly a defensive filter: it beats holding only on structurally declining stocks and misses most of the compounding in quality uptrends. Exits are too early and entries too sparse to stay invested, so it cannot close the idle-capital gap or get near 5-7%/month. Any next idea needs much longer participation in uptrends (trend-following holds, fewer exits) and should rank and fill slots instead of skipping signals. Fix the _RiskOnly.name bug before trusting future runs.
