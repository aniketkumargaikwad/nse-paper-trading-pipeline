# Research notes — 2026-09-12

## Trend-pullback reclaim: EMA50>EMA200 with RSI reclaim of 40

**Training outcome:** 0 of 5 combinations profitable after fees, net 0

**Lessons:** 1) Validate that a rule fires before spending a version on it — count raw signal occurrences on one symbol first, and treat a zero-trade backtest as a bug to fix rather than a result to interpret. 2) Log why entries are rejected (per-condition hit counts) so a dead rule is diagnosable in one run instead of costing a whole version. 3) Always report buy-and-hold alongside net P&L; on this universe hold returns are in the hundreds of percent, so a profitable-looking strategy can still have zero or negative edge. 4) Five symbols on one timeframe is too thin to conclude anything even when trades do fire — widen the universe before drawing inferences.
